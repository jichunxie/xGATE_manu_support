CRC Visium HD preprocessing (raw 10x -> sc_input_crc.RDS / countList_spatial_crc.RDS)
======================================================================================

The bin-to-cell step that turns the raw 10x Visium HD CRC download into the
`sc_input_crc.RDS` and `countList_spatial_crc.RDS` objects consumed by
01_identify_spatial_domains.R follows the bin2cell tutorial:

    https://github.com/Teichlab/bin2cell.git

Reference single-cell dataset used by IRIS for domain deconvolution:
Guimaraes et al. 2024, Nat. Commun. 15:5694 (via CZI CELLxGENE),
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49916-4  (see docs/data_availability.md).

Downstream xGATE pathway activity per subdomain
(colon_pathway_analysis_results_xgate_subdomain.csv) is produced by
../../run_xgate_spatial.py.
